Model study / why logistic regression

Four models, one dataset, honest result.

Each candidate trains on the same 2,400 synthetic URLs and is scored on an independent 2,400-URL test set, plus 5-fold cross-validation. The generator flips 3% of labels on purpose, so roughly 97% is the ceiling.

Training all four models takes a few seconds the first time; results are cached after that.
