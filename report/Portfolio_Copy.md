PhishLens

Short description

Checks links and email text for signs of phishing. Shows why a link may be unsafe and can check many links at once.

Project summary

I made PhishLens to show the signs behind a phishing score. It does not open links during a normal check. It can also make a report for a list of links.

The link model uses sample data. On a small test with 10 phishing links, it found 1. Extra checks for look-alike names raised this to 9. It did not flag 12 safe links in that test. This test is small and does not show how the app will work on all links.
